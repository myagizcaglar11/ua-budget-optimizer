UA Budget Optimizer

How should a launch budget be split across ad networks, DSPs and social networks? Every extra dollar on a network buys installs at a higher CPI, so the best split balances volume against payback.

The trade-off: installs vs. ROAS target

Each point re-solves the split for that target at the current budget and window. A stricter target pushes money to high-ARPU networks, which costs installs.

How it works

Each +25% of spend on a network raises its CPI by 10% (ad networks), 15% (DSPs) or 20% (social), compounding. So installs grow slower than spend. At the best split, one more dollar is worth the same on every network; the solver finds that point exactly. Day 180 ARPU is projected from days 7–90 with a power curve.
